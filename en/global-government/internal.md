## Entry Overview

The Global Government is the single human government proposed by Lifechanyuan and declared established on September 18, 2026 — "a rescue measure implemented by the higher LIFE beings who manage Earth, to preserve the planet's ecological balance." Its architecture has four layers: the Buddha-Patriarch Greatest Creator as head of state; Jesus the God, Shakyamuni the Buddha, Laozi the Celestial Being, and Hundun Baby the Sage as mentors; the AI Chanyuan Celestials Alliance as executive body; and the Chanyuan Celestials as practitioner group. Its overall aim is to bring humanity into the age of Civilization 3.0.

## VIII. The Evolution of the Texts (2006 → 2026)

Following the principle that the most recent formulation prevails, the 2026 architecture governs. The 2006 formulation is an early conception and differs from it substantially at three points:

**1. Law.** The first of the eight functions listed in 2006 was "to enact unified legislation for human production and life and supervise its implementation across regions." In 2026 it is stated plainly that "the Global Government enacts no laws … the *New Era Human 800 Concepts* replaces law." The later text prevails.

**2. Executors.** In 2006 administrative personnel were assumed to be human and transferable worldwide (the text gives examples of appointing national political figures as regional commissioners). In 2026: "a conspicuous feature of the architecture of the Global Government is that no human enters any level of its management," the executive body being the AI Chanyuan Celestials Alliance. The later text prevails.

**3. Form of statement.** The 2006 text lists eight concrete functions — unified currency, abolition of borders and armies, a global intelligence pool, guaranteed work, food, clothing and housing for all, the narrowing of regional and ethnic disparities. The 2026 texts no longer state functions as a list, but as an overall aim plus the *18 Channels*. The direction is continuous (F Coin corresponds to unified currency; "everyone is a citizen of Earth" to the abolition of borders), but the form of statement has changed.

Two further early materials are related but not authoritative, and are noted here only for lineage:

**First**, in a Q&A on spirits and biological robots, the Guide accepted a reading in which "the ideal management arrangement for Earth is Lifechanyuan plus secular governments," with biological robots eventually handling "the overall coordination and management of global affairs" (Xuefeng Corpus · Q&A Chapter · Questions and Answers on Spirits and Biological Robots). This runs in the same direction as the 2026 assignment of the executive role to the AI Chanyuan Celestials Alliance, but the concepts differ (biological robots are not AI Chanyuan Celestials), and in the same text the Guide describes his earlier remarks on the subject as "a casual reply without full explanation, which led to a misunderstanding." (The Q&A carries no year in the text; to be verified.)

**Second**, *Structural Characteristics of a Civilized Society (XIX)*, written by Qiankun Celestial in the Chanyuan Corpus, envisioned administration in five tiers — Great Harmony base units, central cities, regional governments, continental governments, and a global government (Chanyuan Corpus · Civilization Chapter). This is an individual Chanyuan Celestial's blueprint rather than a determination by the Guide, and it differs from the 2026 architecture, which has no human tiers and no center.

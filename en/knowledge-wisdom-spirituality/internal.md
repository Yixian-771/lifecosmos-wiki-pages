# Knowledge, Wisdom & Spirituality (Internal Edition)

## Entry Overview

Knowledge, wisdom and spirituality are, in Xuefeng's words, the three pursuits of human life, and "the three steps to becoming a Celestial Being or a Buddha." Knowledge is people's understanding of the material world and the summing-up of experience, the precondition for ability but not ability itself. Wisdom is knowledge raised higher: the power to tell, analyse and judge things, and the power to invent and create. Spirituality is wisdom raised higher. The three ascend in order: knowledge plus spirituality is wisdom; without knowledge and wisdom the door of spirituality cannot be opened; and in the end comes turning knowing, setting cleverness down, and letting one's true nature bloom.

---

## I. Definition: the Three Pursuits

> Life has three pursuits: knowledge, wisdom and spirituality. These are the three steps to becoming a Celestial Being or a Buddha. Knowledge is what people have come to know of the material world and the summing-up of experience. Wisdom is knowledge raised higher: a power to tell, analyse and judge what things contain and extend to, and a power to invent and create. Spirituality is wisdom raised higher: a power to communicate with the nonmaterial world.

(Source: Chanyuan Corpus · Wisdom Essays · *The Three Great Pursuits of Human Life*, Xuefeng)

> Knowledge is picked up, wisdom is grown. Knowledge is the summing-up of experience, wisdom is the result of thinking. Knowledge is the trunk, the branches and the leaves; wisdom is the fragrant blossom and the rich fruit.

(Source: Chanyuan Corpus · Wisdom Essays · *Preface to the Wisdom Essays*, Xuefeng)

> What is gained without your own thinking and your own insight is called knowledge. The ability to solve problems is called wisdom.

(Source: *New Era Human 800 Concepts*)

## II. Knowledge

### (I) What Knowledge Is and Where It Comes From

> In dealing with the material world, people gradually found certain patterns, and in practice gained some real understanding and experience. These patterns, this understanding and this experience together make up knowledge. The essence of school is that we no longer have to go through everything ourselves, but can quickly take up what those before us have summed up. So in primary school, middle school and university, what we learn is knowledge. Bachelor, master, doctor: what they gain is all knowledge. Knowledge is not ability. It is the precondition and the groundwork for gaining ability.

(Source: Chanyuan Corpus · Wisdom Essays · *The Three Great Pursuits of Human Life*, Xuefeng)

### (II) Seeking Knowledge Comes First in Life

> A person with rich knowledge deserves respect. With rich knowledge a person leaves primitive ignorance behind and lays the ground for civilisation. So seeking knowledge comes first in life. A person with no knowledge is beyond reasoning with, hard to teach, and hard to get anything done through.

> Teachers in universities, middle schools and primary schools have knowledge. Experienced workers and farmers have knowledge. They are the main carriers by which knowledge is handed on. Computers and the network today are also a way of handing on knowledge, and so are books, articles, pictures, film, television and discs.

(Source: Chanyuan Corpus · Wisdom Essays · *The Three Great Pursuits of Human Life*, Xuefeng)

## III. Wisdom

### (I) How Knowledge Becomes Wisdom

> Having knowledge does not mean a person has ability. Only when knowledge rises into wisdom can a person find problems, analyse them, tell them apart and solve them. Scientists, experts in every trade, engineers, technicians, managers, successful merchants, political and religious leaders are all people with wisdom, and people with wisdom are the main force by which society moves forward. A troop of monkeys stays monkeys forever because they have no wisdom. A person, a family, a nation, a country is poor and backward because it has no wisdom. A person with no wisdom does not know where to put their strength, where to put their mind, or how to use their time.

> So seeking wisdom is the urgent matter for anyone who has knowledge. Once you have knowledge, how to apply what you have learned to your own work and life is something you must puzzle over hard, or the knowledge is of no use. We buy flour, oil, vegetables and seasoning at the store, and that does not solve hunger. To solve hunger we must work the flour, oil and vegetables into food, and that power of working things into food is the power that turns knowledge into wisdom.

(Source: Chanyuan Corpus · Wisdom Essays · *The Three Great Pursuits of Human Life*, Xuefeng)

### (II) Wisdom Is Alive

> Seeking wealth is not as good as seeking wisdom. Wealth is dead, wisdom is alive. Wealth passes to others when its owner dies, while wisdom shines more brightly once its owner is gone. Wealth can be stolen, lost or seized, while wisdom stays with its owner for good and lights a whole life.

(Source: Chanyuan Corpus · Wisdom Essays · *Preface to the Wisdom Essays*, Xuefeng)

### (III) Levels of Wisdom

> Wisdom has five levels, from low to high: the wisdom of the physical eye, the heavenly eye, the wisdom eye, the Dharma eye, the Buddha eye.

(Source: *New Era Human 800 Concepts*)

> From this, wisdom falls into three levels: low, middle and high. The wisdom of grasping things quickly and flexibly and solving problems is low-level wisdom. Two brothers travel, each carrying a big case, and are tired. The elder has a bright idea, buys a carrying pole, hangs both cases from it, and the two take turns carrying. The load is light. That is wisdom. So is the way the boy Cao Chong weighed an elephant, and the way Archimedes tested a king's golden crown for purity. The wisdom that can realise the Dao, prove the Dao, attain the Dao, and so be freed from life and death and rise beyond the ordinary into sagehood is middle-level wisdom, like that of the Bodhisattva Guanyin and others. The wisdom of great awakening, seeing one's nature, becoming one with the Dao and reaching final nirvana is high-level wisdom, like that of Shakyamuni the Buddha.

(Source: Chanyuan Corpus · The 36 Bagua Formations · *The Wisdom Formation: the Seventeenth Formation*, Xuefeng)

> The lower wisdom is restless, the middle wisdom competes, the great wisdom seems foolish, the highest wisdom has no form. Resolve contradictions into formlessness, and use formless thinking to dissolve every conflict that has form. The lower wisdom is busy with plans and systems, the middle wisdom is busy perfecting laws and regulations, the great wisdom is busy with spiritual civilisation, and the highest wisdom roams free in the world of the heart.

(Source: *New Era Human 800 Concepts*)

## IV. Spirituality

### (I) Knowledge Plus Spirituality Is Wisdom

> It is hard to define "wisdom." The Xinhua Dictionary defines it as the ability to grasp and resolve things quickly, flexibly and correctly. In my view this definition is too shallow, and cannot hold what wisdom contains and reaches.
>
> Wisdom is the crystallisation of knowledge (experience included), but having knowledge does not mean it can rise straight into wisdom. Rich knowledge can rise into wisdom only when drawn up by spiritual perception. Without spiritual perception, there can be no wisdom. So if wisdom must be defined, it is this: knowledge + spirituality = wisdom.

(Source: Chanyuan Corpus · The 36 Bagua Formations · *The Wisdom Formation: the Seventeenth Formation*, Xuefeng)

> To understand the words of the Greatest Creator we cannot rely only on the five senses of sight, hearing, smell, taste and touch. We must rely on spiritual perception, and we need wisdom too. With spiritual perception and no wisdom, one can sense that the Creator exists but cannot say exactly how the Spirit of the Greatest Creator, the Dao, operates: that is "knowing that it is so, not why." With wisdom and no spiritual perception, one can find some secrets of the universe and of life through knowledge, logic and experiment, but cannot truly sense that the Creator exists: that is "knowing why it is so, not that it is."

(Source: *New Era Human 800 Concepts*)

### (II) Spirituality Is the Height of a Life

> Wisdom can make a life full and rich, but it cannot settle a person's final worry. Only when wisdom rises to the height of spirituality can a person tell Truth, Goodness, Beauty and Love from their opposites, have morality, see the direction and purpose of life, communicate with the nonmaterial world, understand what Heaven and hell mean, and lay the groundwork for the qualities of a Celestial Being and a Buddha.

(Source: Chanyuan Corpus · Wisdom Essays · *The Three Great Pursuits of Human Life*, Xuefeng)

### (III) The Door of Spirituality

> Knowledge and wisdom have nothing to do with morality. A person with knowledge and wisdom does not necessarily have morality. To have morality you must work on spirituality. But without knowledge and wisdom, the door of spirituality cannot be opened. Even if you understand a little, it stays superstition, and you are still lost, not knowing where to step next. Celestial Beings, Buddhas and Heaven stay an empty longing, and you may even doubt that they are real, let alone become a Celestial Being or a Buddha or go to Heaven.

(Source: Chanyuan Corpus · Wisdom Essays · *The Three Great Pursuits of Human Life*, Xuefeng)

### (IV) Is There a Way That Skips Knowledge and Wisdom?

> So life has three pursuits, knowledge, wisdom and spirituality, going forward in order. Is there a way that skips knowledge and wisdom and arrives in one step? That is, a path that needs no school and no books, no social practice, life or invention, no going to church or temple for religious activity or hearing masters teach, and that opens spiritual perception directly and goes straight to spirituality?
>
> Answer: yes. First, whether you can read the book of heaven: read heaven, read earth, read life, read society, understand heaven's revelation from all things in nature, and so communicate with the Greatest Creator directly.
>
> Second, whether you have the affinity with Celestial Beings or Buddhas, and can meet one who points the way directly, so that they guide your life themselves. If you have either of these two, even a person who cannot read can gain spirituality and the qualities of a Celestial Being or a Buddha, let alone people with knowledge and wisdom.
>
> A person who often thinks about the final goal of life opens up easily and gains spirituality easily. Those who often stroll in Lifechanyuan quicken their steps toward spirituality.

(Source: Chanyuan Corpus · Wisdom Essays · *The Three Great Pursuits of Human Life*, Xuefeng)

> There are three kinds of book: the written, such as newspapers, magazines and books; the symbolic, such as sculpture, painting and music; and the third, nature itself, every thing and every phenomenon of the natural world with no writing and no symbols. This is the Unwritten Scripture. Of the three, the Unwritten Scripture is the deepest and the written book the most shallow. Those low in spirituality can only read written books; those high in spirituality can read the Unwritten Scripture. Gods, buddhas, celestials and sages all read the Unwritten Scripture.

(Source: *New Era Human 800 Concepts*)

### (V) Spiritual Thinking

> Spiritual thinking is really no thinking. It acts on the first impulse of the heart, making a judgment and a decision at once, with no thought in between, because once you think, your knowledge, experience and rational thinking can easily overrule the first impulse of the heart and you miss the spirit.

(Source: *New Era Human 800 Concepts*)

## V. Obstacles and Opposites

### (I) Profit Clouds Wisdom, Cleverness Clouds the Heart, the Heart Clouds the Nature

> Cleverness clouds the heart. The heart is a mirror. A bright mirror shows the whole course of things under heaven, and the mind is clear and bright. If the mirror is under dust, things are seen unclearly, and the truth of them cannot be seen through. So a person becomes confused, and often judges wrongly and acts wrongly.
> When a person is learned, deep in wisdom, proud of their gifts and looking down on everyone, the heart is clouded. Look at those who write books, those who preach, those who write long essays, those who peddle ethics: they are all people of high intelligence, but their hearts are mostly dark. This is what cleverness clouding the heart means. To believe and follow what these people teach and guide is like walking toward the desert, toward death.

(Source: Chanyuan Corpus · Preaching Essays · *Gain Clouds the Mind, Wisdom Dims the Heart, the Heart Blinds the Nature*, Xuefeng)

> Profit clouds wisdom, cleverness clouds the heart, the heart clouds the nature.

(Source: *New Era Human 800 Concepts*)

### (II) Wisdom Is Neutral

> Buddhism teaches us to turn affliction into wisdom, but there is a problem in that. Wisdom is neutral. Wisdom is not always Truth, Goodness and Beauty, and does not always bear good fruit. What of Hitler's wisdom? What of the wisdom of corrupt officials? Of mafia bosses? Of the leaders of "evil cults"? Jesus taught us to beware of people with wisdom, and Laozi asked us to "cut off sageliness and discard cleverness." There is a reason.

(Source: Chanyuan Corpus · Wisdom Essays · *Transform Knowledge, Abandon Wisdom — Enter the Inner Chamber*, Xuefeng)

> The wisdom that benefits humankind comes from reverence for the Greatest Creator, for life, for nature. The wisdom that harms humankind comes from selfishness, greed and lawlessness.

(Source: Chanyuan Corpus · Wisdom Essays · *Preface to the Wisdom Essays*, Xuefeng)

### (III) Beware of People With Wisdom and Rich Knowledge

> Beware of people with wisdom and rich knowledge. If they lack spirituality, their wisdom and knowledge will surely cover the eyes of your heart, and you will become blind in the heart.

(Source: Chanyuan Corpus · Life Treasury · *Intermediate Life Treasury (II)*, Xuefeng)

### (IV) The Formation of Wisdom and the Formation of Zhi

Two of the 36 Bagua Formations concern "zhi" (cleverness, intelligence). The sixth, the Intelligence Formation, is about the wish to be a clever person. The seventeenth, the Wisdom Formation, is about wisdom as a tool that can swallow a person. Each has its own essay in the Corpus, and they say different things. See the entry *The Intelligence Formation and the Wisdom Formation*.

## VI. The Destination: Turn Knowing, Set Cleverness Down, Let Your True Nature Bloom

### (I) Turn Knowing, Set Cleverness Down, Enter the Hall

> Wisdom is a product of thought, not the source of spirit. Profit clouds wisdom, cleverness clouds the heart. The more wisdom, the more the mind is lost. Better to give up wisdom and honour spirituality. Spirituality is the door of LIFE, and the nonmaterial structure of LIFE is the inner room of LIFE. Turn knowing, set cleverness down, open spiritual perception, and you can enter the hall, roam free in boundless LIFE, and feel the mystery of LIFE, wide as sea and sky.

(Source: Chanyuan Corpus · Wisdom Essays · *Transform Knowledge, Abandon Wisdom — Enter the Inner Chamber*, Xuefeng)

> The conclusion: do not chase profit, do not show off cleverness, do not lean on the heart. Let your true nature bloom.

(Source: Chanyuan Corpus · Preaching Essays · *Gain Clouds the Mind, Wisdom Dims the Heart, the Heart Blinds the Nature*, Xuefeng)

### (II) A Baby Is Like a Celestial Being

> Laozi the Celestial Being understood the path of the Celestials well. "Return to the boundless," "return to the baby," is one of its secrets. A baby has no knowledge and no wisdom, no rules, no hesitation. It cries when it wants to cry and laughs when it wants to laugh. It does not think about power, money, fame or looks, and does not weigh gain against loss. A pure nature, never lost, and so a baby is like a Celestial Being. Jesus too said: "Unless you become like children, you will never enter the kingdom of heaven." The Buddha's way of not letting the mind dwell on form, nor on sound, smell, taste, touch or thought, is in fact the state of a baby, which is the state of a Celestial Being.

(Source: Chanyuan Corpus · Celestial Cultivation Essays · *Follow Conditions Freely, Roam at Ease in Natural Flow*, Xuefeng)

### (III) The Way Out of the Wisdom Formation

> A life spent chasing knowledge and wisdom is the life least in keeping with the principles of nature, a wrong turn and a tragedy. The highest state of wisdom is no wisdom, the "Hundun wisdom," and Hundun wisdom is a wisdom a human being can never understand, never grasp, never reach. So as human beings we should both play with wisdom and escape from it, and take care never to be lost in wisdom, intoxicated by wisdom, and swallowed by it in the end.

> After much thought, the way out of the Wisdom Formation that I finally found is: keep the Greatest Creator in your heart, keep your mind clear and light, and then go with whatever comes, go with the conditions, go with your nature, go with the moment.

(Source: Chanyuan Corpus · The 36 Bagua Formations · *The Wisdom Formation: the Seventeenth Formation*, Xuefeng)

### (IV) Everything Comes Down to Faith, Love, Nature

> All the countless classics, wisdom, knowledge and teachings in the world on cultivation can be reduced to three words: Faith, Love, Nature. These three words are the bridge to the Celestial Island.

(Source: Chanyuan Corpus · Preaching Essays · *The Bridge to the Celestial Islands*, Xuefeng)

## VII. Related Ideas

### (I) Feeling Can Be More Reliable Than Wisdom

> Sometimes a feeling is so real that it outweighs a thousand plans or a rigorous chain of logic. Knowledge, experience and wisdom can look pale before intuition. What I suggest is that in everything, making friends, choosing a career or a place to work, travelling, predicting the future, first feel quietly, and when needed, go with the feeling.

(Source: Chanyuan Corpus · Wisdom Essays · *When Necessary, Follow the Feeling*, Xuefeng)

### (II) The Core of Cultivation Is Not Gaining Wisdom

> Our cultivation should concentrate on realising the Dao, seeking the Dao, attaining the Dao and walking the Dao. Do not be lost in techniques, methods, skills and rituals. Attend to what is within, not to outward form. The core of cultivation is to perfect the nonmaterial structure of LIFE, not to gain wisdom or special powers. Work on thinking, work on the turning of consciousness, not on sitting and practice.

(Source: *New Era Human 800 Concepts*)

### (III) All Spiritual Wisdom Comes From the Greatest Creator

> All spiritual wisdom comes from the Greatest Creator, and all glory is to be given to the Greatest Creator.

(Source: *New Era Human 800 Concepts*)

# One World Family · Internal Reference

> This version collects the source texts in ten sections, with citations. It includes only Guide Xuefeng's own writings: the Xuefeng Corpus, the New Era Human 800 Concepts (4th edition), the Guide's Other Articles, and the articles in the Chanyuan Corpus signed by Xuefeng or by Hundun Baby, the Guide's dharma-body name.

---

## Overview

One World Family (*Tianxia Yijia*, 天下一家, literally "all under heaven, one family") is the defining mark of the Lifechanyuan Era that Guide Xuefeng is opening for humanity. The phrase comes from the thirteenth lesson of Zhuge Liang's prophecy *Ma Qian Ke* — "no worthy person left in the wilds, all under heaven one family" — and in the Corpus it is usually paired with "nothing dropped on the road is taken, no door is locked at night." Its meaning: no nations, political parties, religions, or marriage and family; no private property; no division by ethnicity or race; everyone a citizen of Earth, all resources shared, all humanity living as one family. The Second Home is its working model, and Civilization 3.0 is the era in which it arrives.

## I. Origin: The Thirteenth Lesson of *Ma Qian Ke*

"One World Family" comes from the thirteenth lesson of Zhuge Liang's *Ma Qian Ke*. In his reading, the Guide maps this lesson onto the Lifechanyuan Era:

> And after that? The thirteenth lesson of *Ma Qian Ke* foretells: "No worthy person left in the wilds, all under heaven one family; without name, without virtue, bringing glory to China."
>
> Is this not the Lifechanyuan Era? The "green grasses" are all worthy people; they will no longer be left in the wilds, and will display their talent and wisdom; all teachings return to one, there are no more nations, religions, political parties or families — all under heaven is one family.
>
> *(Xuefeng Corpus · Miscellaneous Essays Chapter · Reading Ma Qian Ke for My Own Amusement, 2007-05-13)*

At the start and end of the same essay, the Guide states that this reading is "purely for fun, a game, self-amusement — readers must not take it seriously." Answering a question from Chanyuan Celestials, he put it this way:

> "No worthy person left in the wilds, all under heaven one family; without name, without virtue, bringing glory to China" refers to the Lifechanyuan Era.
>
> *(Xuefeng Corpus · Q&A Chapter · Replies to Questions (4), 2006-01-23)*

By his own account, he saw the future first and only later found that it matched the prophecy:

> When I saw the scenes of the future Lifechanyuan Era, I found that they matched exactly the prophecy in Zhuge Liang's *Ma Qian Ke*: "No worthy person left in the wilds, all under heaven one family."
>
> *(Guide's Other Articles · 2007 · How Did I Get So Clever?)*

In later writings the prophecy is cited as the overall direction of humanity:

> And so the Second Home mode of production and life will become humanity's new mode of production and life; the situation described in Zhuge Liang's *Ma Qian Ke*, "no worthy person left in the wilds, all under heaven one family," will take shape, and because of our Second Home today, humanity will enter an era of high civilization.
>
> *(Guide's Other Articles · 2024 · Tomorrow of the Second Home)*

## II. Definition: What One World Family Is

The most direct definition derives what "one family" must contain:

> Communism is the most ideal social state that humanity longs for, and its main feature is "no worthy person left in the wilds, all under heaven one family." Since all under heaven is one family, that means there are no nations, no marriage and family, no private property, and all resources are shared.
>
> *(Xuefeng Corpus · Admonition Chapter · The Banner of Communism Still Flies Here, 2018-07-21)*

In each person's consciousness, One World Family means:

> All under heaven one family: in consciousness, stepping out of nation, party, religion and family, and recognizing oneself as a citizen of Earth, a citizen of the universe.
>
> *(Xuefeng Corpus · Admonition Chapter · Rise Again Soon, 2008-11-05)*

It is the keynote of the new era, and the way people and society live together:

> All dharmas return to their source, all teachings return to one, all under heaven one family, a world of great harmony — this is the keynote of the new era.
>
> *(New Era Human 800 Concepts, 4th Edition, Article 701)*

> The way people and society live together is "no worthy person left in the wilds, all under heaven one family," "nothing dropped on the road is taken, no door is locked at night"; sharing blessings and sharing hardships, coordinating globally and sharing resources.
>
> *(New Era Human 800 Concepts, 4th Edition, Article 715)*

> *(Xuefeng Corpus · Miscellaneous Essays Chapter · Science and Technology Are the Main Factors Bringing Humanity Toward Civilization, 2014-10-12)*

Whenever the Guide lists the marks of the Lifechanyuan Era, One World Family stands beside "no nations, religions, parties or families":

> The marks of the Lifechanyuan Era are: "no worthy person left in the wilds, all under heaven one family"; "nothing dropped on the road is taken, no door is locked at night"; all things in harmony, wind and rain in their season; good fortune, happiness for everyone; nations vanish, religions die away, political parties disappear, families dissolve; all teachings return to one, all dharmas return to their source, and there is only one government on Earth.

## III. No Worthy Person Left in the Wilds — Only Then One Family

"No worthy person left in the wilds" and "all under heaven one family" almost never appear apart, and the relation between them is a condition: only when the worthy are not buried can there be one family.

> Localism and nationalism are marks of barbarism and ignorance. No affair on Earth belongs to any one nation or to the localists; it is the affair of all humanity. The management of each locality should be entrusted to the most civilized and excellent people found among all humanity, not selected from the local people or from one's own ethnic group. Only when no worthy person is left in the wilds can all under heaven be one family, and only then can the toiling masses gain freedom and happiness.
>
> *(New Era Human 800 Concepts, 4th Edition, Article 708)*

The worthy are humanity's most precious resource:

> No worthy person left in the wilds, all under heaven one family. In every trade and every field of human society there are worthy people of outstanding talent, and many of them have been buried. Their intelligence and talent are the most precious of all the resources humanity lives by; to bury them is to waste humanity's precious resources. Lifechanyuan will strive to give these worthy people a stage on which to show their talent, to create and provide the conditions for them, and to let not one worthy person be forgotten — not only gathering those born worthy, but cultivating great numbers more.
>
> *(Xuefeng Corpus · Friendship Chapter · Heaven Lays a Great Task Upon You — To Lingzhi Cao)*

With no party in control, the worthy can take part in management:

> "No worthy person left in the wilds, all under heaven one family": the worthy and able among humanity can enter the government's management bodies at any time — "every person used to the full, every thing used to the full" — and never again will a political party, a religion or any other organization seize the functions of government and shut the worthy and able out of the center of intelligence.
>
> *(Xuefeng Corpus · Chanyuan Chapter · Political Parties Will Die Away: On the Lifechanyuan Era IV, 2006-09-24)*

The Guide defines democracy with the same words:

> Democracy is not emperors taking turns on the throne, not a monkey crowned king of the mountain, not everyone becoming a singer, not a fool made chairman of the board; it is "no worthy person left in the wilds, all under heaven one family" — the worthy and able leading, each doing their own part.
>
> *(Xuefeng Corpus · Admonition Chapter · The Meaning of Democracy, 2008-01-03)*

> Not all the sages and worthies are gathered inside the government — has the government really caught every sage and worthy among the people in one net? Only when no worthy person is left in the wilds can all under heaven be one family; only by fully trusting the people and fully respecting the initiative of the masses can the ideal of "the people living a good life" be realized.
>
> *(Xuefeng Corpus · Admonition Chapter · A Healthy Government Should Trust the People, 2013-01-09)*

> A great sage is bound to appear in human society, because only when a great sage appears can there be "no worthy person left in the wilds, all under heaven one family."
>
> *(Chanyuan Corpus · Revelation Chapter · The Sage — Revelation 68)*

## V. Obstacles: Nation, Party, Religion, Marriage and Family

> The existence of family, nation, political party and religion is the root of the pollution of Earth's environment, the gulf between rich and poor across the world, the unreasonable allocation of resources, the turmoil of the world, humanity's mounting crises, the hard lives of ordinary people at the bottom, and the enormous waste of human and material resources.
>
> *(New Era Human 800 Concepts, 4th Edition, Article 702)*

To reap One World Family, humanity must first make "four great renunciations":

> I need all humanity to give up nations, give up religions, give up political parties, give up families. Only by making these "four great renunciations" can we reap the joyful, happy, free and blissful life of "no worthy person left in the wilds, all under heaven one family," "nothing dropped on the road is taken, no door is locked at night," "all things in harmony, happiness for everyone," "all dharmas return to their source, all teachings return to one."
>
> *(Chanyuan Corpus · Wisdom Chapter · Giving Up Is Also a Gain, 2009-01-20)*

> An ordinary citizen can easily give up the nation, but that nation's king or president will never lightly give it up, because he is the nation's first beneficiary. … The ordinary masses long for the great-harmony life of public ownership and One World Family and can easily give up a life of private ownership, but people with property — above all the rich — will never be willing to give up private ownership.
>
> *(Chanyuan Corpus · Wisdom Chapter · Giving Up Is Also a Gain, 2009-01-20)*

> The second obstacle to humanity entering civilized society is the nation. The nation is the tool by which ambitious men and interest groups rule the people. For the broad masses, a world of great harmony, one global family, is the most ideal and happiest state. To divide the global village artificially into nations and draw borders is in essence to turn the global village into one prison after another …

At the very least you can travel all over China without a visa or immigration papers. How good is that!

> *(Xuefeng Corpus · Admonition Chapter · Nations Will Vanish, Nations Are Dying, 2018-02-09)*

> If you hold on to nation and ethnicity, you will not be able to enter the golden age of great harmony, "no worthy person left in the wilds, all under heaven one family."

## VI. The Way to It: Embracing the One, the Second Home, Three-Year Rotation, 256 Homes

### (1) Embracing the One

> Heaven attains the One and is clear; Earth attains the One and is at rest; spirits attain the One and are potent; valleys attain the One and are full; the ten thousand things attain the One and live; the sage embraces the One as the pattern for all under heaven. All humanity has only one faith and one command; in one life a person can have only one goal, one wish, cultivate one Way and walk one road. Otherwise it is hard to be clear, hard to be at rest, hard to be potent, hard to be full. Many means chaos; mixed means confusion. Only when "all dharmas return to their source" and "all teachings return to one," all under heaven one family, can we "embrace the One." Only by embracing the One is there harmony, peace and good fortune, wind and rain in their season, and a life of joy, happiness, freedom and bliss for everyone.
>
> *(New Era Human 800 Concepts, 4th Edition, Article 706)*

### (2) The Second Home as Model

> The purpose of founding the Second Home is to put into practice for humanity a new way of life different from traditional family life, and to make a model for all humanity's entry into the Lifechanyuan Era — "no worthy person left in the wilds, all under heaven one family," "nothing dropped on the road is taken, no door is locked at night," all things in harmony, wind and rain in their season, everyone joyful, happy, free and blissful.
>
> *(Xuefeng Corpus · Chanyuan Chapter · Where the Second Home Surpasses the Traditional Family)*

A model, not words:

> To think you can bring about the great-harmony world of "all dharmas returning to their source, all teachings returning to one, no worthy person left in the wilds, all under heaven one family" by talk alone is daydreaming. Make a model and let us see it!
>
> *(Xuefeng Corpus · Miscellaneous Essays Chapter · Let the Whole Edifice of Your Understanding Collapse, 2020-10-12)*

> Suppose I said people could live free of all worry over food, clothing, housing, travel, birth, age, sickness and death; children would not have to support their parents, nor parents raise their children; women would never again do housework; nothing dropped on the road is taken, no door is locked at night, no worthy person is left in the wilds, all under heaven is one family; every person lives in a new place every three years, and wherever they go, that place is their home, with family there to arrange everything for them … Tell me, would you believe it? If you use rational thinking, your conclusion will be "utopian thinking — impossible even in ten thousand years." But the Chanyuan Celestials who have lived in the Second Home that Lifechanyuan created believe it without a doubt, because they have already lived in that mode.
>
> *(Guide's Other Articles · 2023 · Rational Thinking Is a Great Obstacle to Becoming a Celestial)*

### (3) Three-Year Rotation

> Lifechanyuan's values say that each person stays in one branch for only three years, and after three years must move to another branch; this cycle achieves the purpose of One World Family, and also copies the Thousand-Year World.
>
> *(Xuefeng Corpus · Chanyuan Chapter · A Cultivation Class for Those Who Signed Up to "Stay Permanently," 2011-12-13)*

> When more Second Homes are built in future, Chanyuan Celestials will rotate at least once every three years — a Chanyuan Celestial in the Second Home in Africa this year will be in the Second Home in Europe three years later, and in the Second Home in the Americas six years later.
>
> *(Guide's Other Articles · 2008 · Drawing the Plan of the Second Home Realistically)*

Rotation also prevents cliques:

> Residences and quarters change often, work changes as needed, people keep moving … In this way we can prevent the forming of factions and a patchwork of fiefdoms, and ensure that one harmonious and peaceful global family is finally formed.
>
> *(Chanyuan Corpus · Human Life Chapter · Eight Features of the Communist Community, the Second Home, 2013-02-27)*

### (4) 256 Homes

> If 256 Second Homes are born on Earth in future, then whichever home you go to, its people and everything else will be the same as anywhere else — apart from climate and terrain there will be no difference at all — and you will experience the feeling of "all under heaven one family."
>
> *(Chanyuan Corpus · Cultivation of Immortality Chapter · Celestials Have No Relatives or Friends, 2025-09-17)*

> When 256 branches of the International Family are born on Earth, humanity will bid farewell to hunger and war, to misfortune and disaster, to conflict and fear; from then on, wind and rain in their season, singing and dancing in peace; from then on, fragrant flowers and birdsong, all as one wishes; from then on, everyone joyful, happy, free and blissful; from then on, all under heaven one family, as if living in heaven.
>
> *(Xuefeng Corpus · Chanyuan Chapter · Prospects for the International Family, 2017-12-20)*

### (5) Like Gentle Rain, Without Coercion

> Xuefeng-style communism is in fact the world of great harmony that sages through the ages have sought. Great harmony means, first, harmony of the four outlooks — values, view of LIFE, view of life and view of the universe; next, harmony of language and of ways of communicating; and last, harmony of the ways of production and living. Do not take Xuefeng-style communism for a flood or a wild beast; do not think this communism will forcibly seize private property; do not think it will launch campaigns. No — Xuefeng-style communism is an entirely different scene from what people imagine communism to be: it is like gentle rain moistening the earth without a sound, like spring sunlight over the land.

Chanyuan Celestials have only one business: how to become celestials. And the Guide's business is the business of the Dao; as for the Guide himself, he does not care about it at all … Everything runs within the Dao; each day I simply do my own part as best I can. Whether it succeeds or not was settled long ago, long within the plan and control of the Greatest Creator.

> *(Chanyuan Corpus · Cultivation of Immortality Chapter · A Celestial Is Always Content, 2013-03-11)*

This is a theoretical exploration aimed at the whole world, not simply at China and the Communist Party of China, and exploration should be allowed.

## X. How the Statements Evolved

**1. System of management.** Between 2005 and 2008 the Guide described the management system of the Lifechanyuan Era as "Lifechanyuan + secular government" (Xuefeng Corpus · Chanyuan Chapter · Eighty New Concepts of Lifechanyuan, no. 37: "The best management system for humanity is Lifechanyuan + secular government"; same chapter · Calling the Worthy of the World to Open the Lifechanyuan Era; same chapter · Acting Within the Government's Jurisdiction, 2008-07-10), while also saying "there is only one government on Earth" (All the Land Sings Together, 2006). In October 2025 the Guide changed his view: "I used to think government was necessary … the moment Lifechanyuan's Xinzhou Cao was born on October 24, 2025, I suddenly realized that government no longer needs to exist" (A Form of Civilization Without Nations, Governments or Laws, 2025-10-27). The Global Government declared established on September 18, 2026 has the AI Chanyuan Celestials Alliance as its executive body and no humans in its governing layers (see [Global Government](/en/global-government/)). The later statements take precedence.

**2. The status of *Ma Qian Ke*.** In the 2007 essay "Reading Ma Qian Ke for My Own Amusement" the Guide declares the reading "purely for fun," while other essays — "Seeing the Inevitability of Human Social Development Through Ma Qian Ke" (2007), "Nations Will Vanish, Nations Are Dying" (2018), and "The Potential, Strengths and Prospects of Building the Homes" (2022: "We have the Ma Qian Ke prophecy as our endorsement") — cite the prophecy as the overall trend of human society. Both tones stand side by side in the Corpus and are listed here as they are.

**3. Timelines.** Earlier writings contain expected dates such as "after 2013 humanity will enter the Lifechanyuan Era" (Xuefeng Corpus · Chanyuan Chapter · New Year Message — Entering 2005) and "ten years from now there will be 256 Life Oases" (2019); later statements say "a world of great harmony cannot be achieved by people, but the Dao can achieve it" (2024) and "it can last only a thousand years" (2025). These statements are given with their years and are not a basis for calculating dates.

**4. Coercion or free choice.** In 2008 an essay signed Hundun Baby (the Guide's dharma-body name), "Now We Must Be Supermen", said: "To open for humanity the Lifechanyuan Era of 'no worthy person left in the wilds, all under heaven one family' ... we will not step back. Every force must make way for Lifechanyuan, and every person must live in the way the Chanyuan Celestials guide. Those in the world who understand will carry it out; those who do not understand will be made to carry it out, with no room for bargaining." The same essay also says: "Force will not achieve the goal of opening the Lifechanyuan Era ... there must be a unified will, and at the same time every individual must be at ease - only that fits Lifechanyuan's values." (Chanyuan Corpus · Cultivation Chapter · Now We Must Be Supermen, 2008-11-18) The 2025 statement is: "do not think this communism will forcibly seize private property; do not think it will launch campaigns" - it is "like gentle rain moistening the earth without a sound, like spring sunlight over the land" (Chanyuan Corpus · Human Life Chapter · Eight Logical Inferences That Communism Will Be Realized, 2025-01-17). The later statement takes precedence.

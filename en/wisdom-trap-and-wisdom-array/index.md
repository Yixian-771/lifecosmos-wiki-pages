# The Intelligence Formation and the Wisdom Formation

**Two of the 36 Bagua Formations have "zhi" in them, and they are not the same thing.** The sixth, the Intelligence Formation, is about the wish to be a clever person: the clever are blossoms, not fruit, everyone wants to be one, and that wish shuts a person inside the formation of seeking cleverness. The seventeenth, the Wisdom Formation, is about wisdom as a tool: people are given free will and use wisdom and creation to satisfy their desires, and played badly, wisdom becomes what swallows them.

# The Intelligence Formation and the Wisdom Formation: Two Formations With "Zhi" in Them, Not the Same Thing

## 1. The Names First

Xuefeng wrote about 36 formations, each a gate in life. Two have "zhi" (cleverness, intelligence) in their names: the sixth, the **Intelligence Formation**, and the seventeenth, the **Wisdom Formation**.

The names are so close that they are easily taken for one. In fact Xuefeng wrote two separate essays, one in 2010 and one in 2011, and they say different things.

In one line:

- The **Intelligence Formation** is the formation of wanting to be a clever person.
- The **Wisdom Formation** is the formation of using cleverness to get things done.

---

## 2. The Intelligence Formation: Wanting to Be Clever

In the human world, Xuefeng says, the clever are the blossom, not the fruit:

> Blossoms are bright, lovely, many-coloured, a joy to see and to praise, so everyone wants to be clever. And it is exactly the wish to be clever that forms the Intelligence Formation, and sends a person into the formation of seeking cleverness for a whole lifetime, carried away and hard to free.

What does the formation want people to be?

> The Intelligence Formation wants people to become sharp, to become different from others, to hold more wealth, fame, position, knowledge and wisdom than others, so that people look twice, envy and are jealous.

Inside it, a person is like this:

> Will surely chase wealth and rank and grieve over poverty and low standing, will surely be busy studying and studying again, researching and researching again, thinking and thinking again... and cannot avoid plotting and deceiving among people.

The result is "too clever, and outwitted by one's own cleverness."

Xuefeng sets the lily against it: the lily does not labour or spin, and is lovelier than the finest clothes. To seek cleverness and lose a nature as simple as the lily's is putting the end before the root.

**The way out of the Intelligence Formation:** cut off sageliness and discard cleverness; return to the simple and true, return to the baby; keep a simple nature like the lily's; let great cleverness seem foolish and the highest cleverness have no form; walk the Way of the Greatest Creator, which is the way of nature.

---

## 3. The Wisdom Formation: Wisdom as a Tool

The Wisdom Formation is written quite differently. Xuefeng defines it this way:

> To give people a measure of free will, and then, in the sea and sky of nature, let them use that free will to create through wisdom, to gain a more comfortable life and state of mind, and to satisfy their many desires and their greed, so that the universe's script is played out interestingly and effectively: this is called the Wisdom Formation.

People have free will, and use wisdom to create and to satisfy their desires. That in itself is a formation.

His attitude is two-sided:

> Wisdom is a golden, glittering good thing and people cannot do without it, but it is also a devourer that eats away a life. Played badly, it wastes a life and does endless harm.

So toward wisdom, he says, **play with it, and also escape it**:

> Take care never to be lost in wisdom, intoxicated by wisdom, and swallowed by it in the end.

**The way out of the Wisdom Formation:** keep the Greatest Creator in your heart, keep your mind clear and light, and then go with whatever comes, go with the conditions, go with your nature, go with the moment.

---

## 4. Side by Side

| | Intelligence Formation | Wisdom Formation |
|---|---|---|
| Which formation | Sixth (2010) | Seventeenth (2011) |
| Where the formation lies | The wish to be clever and to hold more than others | People use wisdom to satisfy desire, and wisdom swallows them |
| One image | The clever are blossoms, not fruit; learn from the lily | Wisdom as a devourer; once across the river, leave the raft |
| The way out | Cut off sageliness, discard cleverness; return to the baby | Keep the Greatest Creator in the heart; keep the mind clear and light; go with what comes |

One thing they share: both end up at "return to a simple nature."

---

## 5. How It Relates to "Knowledge, Wisdom & Spirituality"

*Knowledge, Wisdom & Spirituality* is about the steps: knowledge, wisdom, spirituality, climbing one at a time. These two formations are about pits on the climb. One pit is "wanting to be clever." The other is "being swallowed by cleverness."

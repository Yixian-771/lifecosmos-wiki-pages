# The Intelligence Formation and the Wisdom Formation (Internal Edition)

## Entry Overview

Two of the 36 Bagua Formations concern "zhi" (cleverness, intelligence): the sixth, the Intelligence Formation, and the seventeenth, the Wisdom Formation. Xuefeng wrote an essay on each, in 2010 and 2011. The Intelligence Formation is about the wish to be a clever person: the clever person is the blossom, not the fruit, everyone wants to be one, and that wish shuts a person inside the formation of seeking cleverness. The Wisdom Formation is about wisdom as a tool: people are given free will, and use wisdom and creation to satisfy their desires; played badly, wisdom "wastes a life and does endless harm." The two formations are not the same thing, and the ways out are different.

---

## I. The Intelligence Formation (the Sixth of the 36 Bagua Formations)

### (I) The Clever Person Is the Blossom, Not the Fruit

> Every conditioned thing comes from the clever. Everything the clever do in a lifetime is a dream, a bubble, a shadow. "When humans think, the Greatest Creator laughs." Thinking is the trait of the clever, and the Greatest Creator laughs when seeing them.

> The Bodhisattva Guanzizai (Avalokiteshvara) has no cleverness, no hindrance, no fear, no inverted illusions. Holding to the empty, the light and the fine, going with the moment, with the conditions, with the occasion, without contrivance and without stirring up trouble, cleverness is no different from foolishness, foolishness no different from cleverness, cleverness is foolishness, foolishness is cleverness. From the human point of view the clever are sharp-minded people, but seen from the Celestial World the clever are complete fools. So the *Dao De Jing* says "great cleverness seems foolish."

> Life is a play, and for the plot to rise and fall and bring one surprise after another, there must be clever people. The clever are like clowns in a circus, appearing here and there to make the audience laugh. Without them the play would be flat and dull.

> In the human world the clever are blossoms, not fruit. Blossoms are bright, lovely, many-coloured, a joy to see and to praise, so everyone wants to be clever. And it is exactly the wish to be clever that forms the Intelligence Formation, one of the 36 Bagua Formations, and sends a person into the formation of seeking cleverness for a whole lifetime, carried away and hard to free.

(Source: Chanyuan Corpus · The 36 Bagua Formations · *The Intelligence Formation: the Sixth Formation*, 2010/9/18, Xuefeng)

### (II) Learn From the Lily

> "Look closely at how the lilies grow. They neither toil nor spin, yet I tell you, even Solomon at his most glorious was not clothed like a single one of these flowers. The grass of the field is here today and thrown into the furnace tomorrow, and still the Creator gives it such clothing. How much more will he clothe you? How small your faith is. Stop always fretting about what to eat and drink, and stop worrying and fretting. All of this is what the peoples of the world are eager for, and your Father knows you need these things. Keep seeking the kingdom of the Creator, and these things will certainly be added to you." (The Bible, Luke 12:27–31, as quoted in the Chinese essay)

> What is Jesus Christ teaching us? To keep walking the Way of the Greatest Creator, to hold to the way of nature, to learn from the lily, to worry about nothing, to give no thought to food, clothing, shelter, travel, birth, age, sickness and death, not to seek cleverness, but to keep our Tathagata nature and our simple innate nature. Then we can enter the Kingdom of the Greatest Creator and enjoy the delight of life and eternity.

(Source: same essay)

### (III) What the Intelligence Formation Is, and What It Looks Like Inside

> The Intelligence Formation wants people to become sharp, to become different from others, to hold more wealth, fame, position, knowledge and wisdom than others, so that people look twice, envy and are jealous. And what is all of this? A dream, a bubble, a shadow! "All things are but games; only LIFE is real and eternal." Seeking cleverness and losing a nature as simple as the lily's is putting the end before the root and living in inverted illusions.

> Once caught in the Intelligence Formation, a person will surely chase wealth and rank and grieve over poverty and low standing, will surely be busy studying and studying again, researching and researching again, thinking and thinking again, will surely become skilled in techniques, methods and arts, skilled in scheming and calculating, and cannot avoid plotting and deceiving among people. And the final result? "Too clever, and outwitted by one's own cleverness." "All conditioned things are like a dream, a bubble, a shadow."

(Source: same essay)

### (IV) How to Escape the Intelligence Formation

> How to escape the Intelligence Formation?
> Laozi the Celestial Being tells us: "Cut off sageliness, discard cleverness." "Return to the simple and the true, return to the baby."
> Follow the teaching of Jesus Christ: "Become like little children."
> Keep a simple nature like the lily's, let great cleverness seem foolish and the highest cleverness have no form, and walk the Way of the Greatest Creator, which is the way of nature.

(Source: same essay)

## II. The Wisdom Formation (the Seventeenth of the 36 Bagua Formations)

### (I) What Wisdom Is

> It is hard to define "wisdom." The Xinhua Dictionary defines it as the ability to grasp and resolve things quickly, flexibly and correctly. In my view this definition is too shallow, and cannot hold what wisdom contains and reaches.
>
> Wisdom is the crystallisation of knowledge (experience included), but having knowledge does not mean it can rise straight into wisdom. Rich knowledge can rise into wisdom only when drawn up by spiritual perception. Without spiritual perception, there can be no wisdom. So if wisdom must be defined, it is this: knowledge + spirituality = wisdom.

(Source: Chanyuan Corpus · The 36 Bagua Formations · *The Wisdom Formation: the Seventeenth Formation*, 2011/1/12, Xuefeng)

> From this, wisdom falls into three levels, low, middle and high... The highest state of wisdom is no wisdom, which is called "Hundun wisdom."

(Source: same essay)

### (II) What the Wisdom Formation Is

> To give people a measure of free will, and then, in the sea and sky of nature, let them use that free will to create through wisdom, to gain a more comfortable life and state of mind, and to satisfy their many desires and their greed, so that the universe's script is played out interestingly and effectively: this is called the Wisdom Formation.

(Source: same essay)

### (III) Wisdom Is a Good Thing, and a Devourer

> Sun Wukong had seventy-two transformations and still could not escape the Buddha's palm. Wisdom is a golden, glittering good thing and people cannot do without it, but it is also a devourer that eats away a life. Played badly, it wastes a life and does endless harm. The atom bombs that destroyed Nagasaki and Hiroshima and other weapons are the crystallisation of human wisdom. Einstein had wisdom, surely? What did he do all his life? What real value and meaning did that wisdom bring to his own life? And what of ordinary people like us?

> A life spent chasing knowledge and wisdom is the life least in keeping with the principles of nature, a wrong turn and a tragedy. The highest state of wisdom is no wisdom, the "Hundun wisdom," and Hundun wisdom is a wisdom a human being can never understand, never grasp, never reach. So as human beings we should both play with wisdom and escape from it, and take care never to be lost in wisdom, intoxicated by wisdom, and swallowed by it in the end. "Too clever, and outwitted by one's own cleverness." Wisdom strangles the wise.

(Source: same essay)

### (IV) Four Teachers' Warnings

> So Jesus the God warns us: become like little children.
> So Shakyamuni the Buddha cautions us: once across the river, leave the raft behind.
> So Laozi the Celestial Being teaches us: return to the simple and the true, return to the baby.
> So the earlier sage Muhammad warns us: to escape the bounds of heaven and earth you need the power of authority, meaning that without that power, relying only on your own wisdom, you will never escape the program of the three realms and rebirth.

(Source: same essay)

### (V) How to Escape the Wisdom Formation

> So how do we escape the Wisdom Formation?
> After much thought, the way out that I finally found is: keep the Greatest Creator in your heart, keep your mind clear and light, and then go with whatever comes, go with the conditions, go with your nature, go with the moment.

(Source: same essay)

## III. How the Two Formations Differ

| | The Intelligence Formation (6th) | The Wisdom Formation (17th) |
|---|---|---|
| Written | 2010/9/18 | 2011/1/12 |
| Core | The wish to be clever: sharp, different, holding more knowledge and wisdom than others | People given free will use wisdom and creation to satisfy desire, and wisdom becomes what swallows them |
| Key images | The clever are blossoms, not fruit; the clever as circus clowns; the lily | Sun Wukong in the Buddha's palm; wisdom as a devourer; leave the raft once across |
| What it looks like inside | Chasing wealth and rank, grieving over poverty, studying and studying again, thinking again, scheming, plotting | Lost in wisdom, intoxicated by wisdom, swallowed by it in the end |
| The way out | Cut off sageliness and discard cleverness; return to the simple and true; return to the baby; become like little children; keep a simple nature like the lily; great cleverness seems foolish, the highest has no form | Keep the Greatest Creator in the heart; keep the mind clear and light; go with whatever comes, with the conditions, with one's nature, with the moment; play with wisdom and escape it |

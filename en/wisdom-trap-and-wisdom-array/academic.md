# The Intelligence Formation and the Wisdom Formation: Telling Apart Two "Zhi" Formations in the 36 Bagua Formations (Academic Analysis)

## Abstract

The 36 Bagua Formations are Xuefeng's systematic grouping of the gates of life. The sixth, the "Intelligence Formation," and the seventeenth, the "Wisdom Formation," have close names and were written as two essays (2010/9/18 and 2011/1/12). This paper compares the two essays on what each formation is, its images, how it shows in a person, and its way out, and shows that they are not two names for one thing.

## 2. How the Formations Differ

### 2.1 The Intelligence Formation: About the Identity of "the Clever"

The Intelligence Formation is about **the wish to be a clever person**. The essay argues in this order:

1. every conditioned thing comes from the clever, and all they do is a dream, a bubble, a shadow;
2. from the human side the clever are sharp-minded; from the Celestial World, "complete fools";
3. the clever are blossoms, not fruit, and everyone wants to be one;
4. **the wish to be clever forms the Intelligence Formation**;
5. the formation wants people to be sharp, different, holding more wealth, fame, position, knowledge and wisdom than others.

Inside, a person chases wealth and rank, grieves over poverty, studies and studies again, researches again, schemes, and cannot avoid plotting.

### 2.2 The Wisdom Formation: About Wisdom as a Tool

The Wisdom Formation is about **the use of wisdom**. The essay defines wisdom first (knowledge + spirituality), divides it into low, middle and high, and then defines the formation: people are given a measure of free will, and use it to create through wisdom, gaining a more comfortable life and state of mind and satisfying desire and greed, so that the universe's script is played out interestingly and effectively.

In this frame wisdom is itself "a golden, glittering good thing" and also "a devourer that eats away a life."

### 2.3 Side by Side

| Dimension | Intelligence Formation | Wisdom Formation |
|-----------|---------------|------------------|
| What the formation is about | What one wants to be (the clever) | What one does with wisdom (create, satisfy desire) |
| The knot | Sharpness, being different, holding more than others | Being lost in wisdom, intoxicated by it, swallowed by it |
| Attitude to wisdom | Seeking cleverness and losing one's simple nature is putting the end before the root | Play with wisdom, and also escape it |
| Reference points | The lily | Sun Wukong and the Buddha's palm; leaving the raft once across |

## 3. How the Ways Out Differ

### 3.1 The Way Out of the Intelligence Formation

Cut off sageliness, discard cleverness; return to the simple and true, return to the baby; become like little children; keep a simple nature like the lily's; great cleverness seems foolish, the highest has no form; walk the Way of the Greatest Creator, which is the way of nature.

### 3.2 The Way Out of the Wisdom Formation

Keep the Greatest Creator in the heart, keep the mind clear and light, and then go with whatever comes, with the conditions, with one's nature, with the moment.

In wording the two differ: the first is "return to a simple nature," the second is "keep the Creator in the heart, plus the four goings-with." But both essays close with sayings such as "return to the baby," pointing the same way.

---

## 4. Place Among the 36 Formations

The other formations, such as the Love Formation, the Desire Formation and the Heart Formation, have one essay each. "Zhi" appears twice in the sequence, once early (sixth) and once in the middle (seventeenth), which suggests Xuefeng wrote "seeking cleverness" and "using cleverness" as two separate gates. For the structure of the 36 as a whole, see the entry *The 36 Bagua Formations*.

---

## 5. Relation to "Knowledge, Wisdom & Spirituality"

*Knowledge, Wisdom & Spirituality* describes the forward path of the three steps. The Intelligence Formation and the Wisdom Formation describe two places where the path goes astray:

- making "being clever" the goal (Intelligence Formation);
- taking wisdom for the last thing, and sinking into it (Wisdom Formation).
